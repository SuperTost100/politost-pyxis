The Pyxis mark, star trails. `-dark` files have `#eceef3` trails (dark theme `ink`) and `#f4b942` star for dark grounds; `-light` files have `#15181e` trails and `#b7790c` star for light grounds; `-mono` is solid black for one-colour use. Lockups carry the wordmark "Pyxis" in Figtree 700, outlined. App icons: white trails and gold star on `#3262db` (`primary`); use `-small` below 32px. PNG app icons at 1024, 512, 256, 128 (from the full mark) and 64, 32, 16 (from the small mark). Use the 1024 PNG as the Electron build icon source.
